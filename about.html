Our story

Two decades of hardware. Five years of software. One team.

Vercetti Tech Solutions was built on more than 20 years of hands-on hardware expertise, and has spent the last five building that same discipline into software, web and AI engineering. That combination is rare — most technology partners specialise in one side or the other. We work as an extension of your business, scoping honestly, building carefully, and staying reachable long after launch.

Our head office is in London, United Kingdom, and our delivery team works with clients across India, having completed hundreds of projects across every solution we offer.
